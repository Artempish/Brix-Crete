# Job photos

Drop real project photography in this folder, then register each file in
`data/photos.js`. Until a photo is registered, the site renders drawn artwork
in its place — it will never show stock imagery of someone else's work.

## Naming

    <service>-<town>[-<n>].jpg      driveway-lombard.jpg, patio-hinsdale-2.jpg
    <service>-<town>-before.jpg     driveway-lombard-before.jpg
    <service>-<town>-after.jpg      driveway-lombard-after.jpg

## Sizing

Export at **1600px on the long edge**, JPEG quality ~80. Anything larger is
wasted bytes on a phone; anything smaller looks soft on a retina screen.

Shoot the finished work in flat light (overcast is ideal — direct sun blows out
concrete and hides the finish). Get one wide shot showing the whole slab in
context and one close shot showing the finish texture and a joint.

## Before/after pairs

Shoot the "before" from the same spot and the same height as the "after". The
pair only works if the framing matches.
